## Notes captured during Step 0 (2026-11-08)

- KB scope is limited to what is officially published. Two **public-documentation limitations** found so far (report these
  as limitations of the available public corpus, not as policy gaps):
  1. The NCEMA founding instrument (Federal Decree-Law No. 2 of 2011) is published on the official platform in Arabic only.
  2. No published operational protocol for issuing, verifying or cancelling a national public alert was found; the
     public TDRA material located is a handset channel/behaviour table (server date Dec 2021).
- In-force competence for the public warning system: Federal Decree by Law No. (8) of 2025, Art. (4)(5), "Establish a
  public warning system for the population against potential hazards, in coordination with" NCEMA (in force 01 Jan 2026).
  It repealed Decree-Law No. (35) of 2024, whose Art. (3)(8) had placed this competence with the Civil Defence Authority
  inside NCEMA. Statements about this must cite the article, and must not characterise how institutions operate in practice.
- The UAE platform states that its English texts are translations and that the Arabic text prevails; every citation
  record carries that flag.
