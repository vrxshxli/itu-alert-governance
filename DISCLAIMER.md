# Disclaimer

- **Hypothetical scenario.** The incident simulated in this project is fictional. It is inspired only by the general class
  of public-alert failures. It does not describe, reconstruct or speculate about any real incident, and it does not
  model defence, threat-detection or military systems.
- **No real internal information.** All institutional behaviour in the simulation is generated from published official
  documents. Nothing reflects any institution's internal procedures, communications or decisions.
- **Not an assessment of any country or institution.** The project tests whether a published-policy knowledge base
  supports accountable, evidence-grounded coordination. Where the published corpus does not establish a responsibility,
  the system reports that as a limitation of the available public documents, not as a finding about how any
  institution operates.
- **Not legal or official advice.** Outputs are research artefacts produced with AI models and may contain errors. English
  versions of UAE legislation are translations; the Arabic text prevails. English renderings produced by this project
  are unofficial.
- **No personal data.** The project processes no personal data. Scenario events contain no real persons.
